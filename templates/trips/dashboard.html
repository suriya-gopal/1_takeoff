How the numbers are worked out

  • Response time is measured around the whole AI call. Requests that were refused or rate limited never reach the model and are left out of the timing and cost figures.
  • Spread is the standard deviation divided by the mean; a low value means response times are consistent. Slow outliers are requests above Q3 + 1.5 x IQR.
  • Cost is an estimate. The on-device model is priced by the seconds it ran on a GPU server (${{ server_cost_per_hour }} per hour); the hosted model by prompt and completion tokens at the provider's price per million tokens.
  • Topics come from keywords in the question. Individual questions are never shown here.
